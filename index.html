About Out There

Live outside. Play dirty. Stay fresh.

Built by Nyjah Huston, one of the best skateboarders in the world.

Out There is personal care for people who live hard, push limits and refuse to sit still. Nyjah founded the brand, built the products, and uses them himself. Every formula is made to hold up in real conditions: heat, sun, travel, long days and longer nights. If it doesn’t perform when you’re actually out there, it doesn’t make the cut.

“This vision comes straight from how I live. Skating in the streets with my friends, getting lost in nature, hitting trails, hiking up rocks, riding dirtbikes through the hills.”

Nyjah Huston  Pro Skateboarder, Out There Founder
Why Out There Is Different
PERFORMANCE
Up to 48-hour odor protection, made to last through workouts, heat, travel and repeat use
PLANT-POWERED
Probiotic-derived ingredients balance odor-causing bacteria instead of just masking smell
CLEAN BY DESIGN
No aluminum, parabens, phthalates, PEGs, sulfates, silicones or artificial dyes
COLOGNE-GRADE SCENT
Layered, designer-inspired notes like shiso, vetiver and amber, not the loud, sweet sport scent most guys’ deodorant smells like

Out There is a deodorant, not an antiperspirant: it neutralizes odor and absorbs moisture without blocking your sweat glands.

Switching from an aluminum antiperspirant? Your skin can take 1 to 4 weeks to rebalance, and performance improves as your body adjusts.

Stick or Spray

Choose the stick or the spray, whichever fits how you move. Both run on the same Clean Body formula.

Zinc Ricinoleate

A zinc salt from castor oil that traps and neutralizes odor molecules at the source instead of covering them with fragrance.

Fermented Radish Root

A probiotic ferment that keeps odor-causing bacteria in check while supporting a healthy skin microbiome.

Tapioca, Aloe & Sea Moss

Plant starches absorb moisture for a dry, comfortable finish, while aloe and sea moss soothe skin through heat and friction.

The Scent

Skip the cologne

Scent is what sets Out There apart. Every scent is designer-inspired, drawn from nature and landscapes around the world, and built to feel refined, grounded and modern, never overpowering. It sits close to your skin like a good cologne instead of filling the room, so people notice you smell good, even after a long day outside.

Content Guide — Get Out There

Who you’re talking to: sporty, outdoorsy guys who are always moving, from the skatepark and the surf to the trail and the gym.

CONCEPT 01

The Sweat Test

Put it on before your real activity (skate, surf, ride, climb or train) and check in after. Show the application, the activity, and how you feel and smell at the end.

CONCEPT 02

Built by Nyjah

Tell the story behind it: pro skater Nyjah Huston built Out There for people who live outside the lines. Connect it to how you live and why that matters to you.

CONCEPT 03

The Fragrance Review

Review it like a cologne. Show your fragrance collection, talk about the notes you’re usually into, then show where your Out There scent fits in: what hits first, how it wears through the day, and why it doesn’t smell like typical deodorant.

03

Do’s & Don’ts

DO

  • +Say “founded by pro skater Nyjah Huston” in your hook
  • +Show the product up close and going on clear, not just the packaging
  • +Film outdoors, in motion, in good light
  • +Talk to the camera like you’re FaceTiming a friend
  • +Add subtitles. Most people watch on mute
  • +Flip the video so “Out There” isn’t backwards

DON’T

  • −Read a script or sound like an ad
  • −Call it an antiperspirant or say it stops sweat
  • −Promise more than 48 hours, or make health claims
  • −Put words in Nyjah’s mouth
  • −Trash other brands

Won’t be accepted: faceless videos, product-only shots with no real use, or a flash-sale angle.

05

Video Formats

Pick a format, then fill it with your talking points.

FORMAT 01

How I Use It

What works
  • Outdoors, step by step
  • Bold on-screen text
  • Show it going on clear
FORMAT 02

Talk-to-Camera Review

What works
  • Hook in the first second
  • Honest, casual take
  • Show your cologne shelf, then where your scent fits
FORMAT 03

Why I Switched

What works
  • Toss your old deodorant on camera
  • Quick pop-up cutaways: stains, the stick, Nyjah
  • Word-by-word captions
MORE IDEAS
The Sweat Test

Put it on, skate, surf or train, then do a smell check after.

The Long Day Out

On in the morning, follow your day outside, smell check at night.
